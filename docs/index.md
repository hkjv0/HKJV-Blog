# :lucide-fingerprint-pattern: Introduction 
!!! warning "Disclaimers"
    - Although I try to do thorough research before recommending anything, it’s important to note that, like many in this “community,” I’m still learning which is why I strongly recommend that you do your own research to determine whether the tools you see recommended are right for your needs and whether they live up to their claims.
    - Much of the content/most of which is in English has been translated using tools such as Deepl; however, none of the information provided on this page was created by artificial intelligence.

## :lucide-book-open: About this site
On this site, you'll find guides based on what I've learned over time, blog posts, and more. I recommend checking out my main social media accounts (Mastodon and Twitter) for additional information! Also, if you have any corrections, questions, or suggestions, feel free to send them to me through one of my contact methods.

!!! note "Editorial priorities"
    The blog and guides follow this priority: Security first, then privacy. For example, a Chromium-based browser such as Trivalent—or even Chrome itself with additional security hardening—will be recommended over Firefox-based browsers, since without security, there can be no privacy, and in the case of browsers, a Chromium-based browser can actually offer privacy.

## :lucide-square-user-round: About me
Hi, I'm HKJV, to summarize who I am, I can say the following: In mid-2025, I became interested in digital privacy and cybersecurity after watching a documentary about Edward Snowden and learning about other investigations into attacks on digital privacy—attacks that have only continued to increase since then. From that moment on, I decided to devote much of my time to protecting my own privacy and that of my loved ones using the tools at my disposal. Many of my views on digital security and privacy that you’ll find on this page and on social media have been largely influenced by these wonderful projects:
